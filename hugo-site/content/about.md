# About Ali Al-Kaswan

**Researching the application of Machine Learning to Software Engineering.** Specifically interested in **Natural Language Processing** to help in Software Engineering Tasks and the intersection of **Machine Learning** and **Security**. 

My previous research involved using Pre-Trained Transformers to help with Reverse Engineering Binaries. I am currently focussing on the privacy of Large Language Models.

## Studies

I previously obtained my Master's in Computer Science and Engineering with a specialization in Cyber Security from the Delft University of Technology. My [Masters Thesis](http://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:06a525ef-a9a8-4899-bbdb-cf2925808dae) involved using Transformer models to summarise Decompiled Binaries. I have also obtained my Bachelor's in Computer Science and Engineering from the same university, where my [Bachelors Thesis](http://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:240aacf2-51c0-46c9-a3c2-5994a3321004) involved making an [inclusive game](https://datacentergame.nl/) to make Datacenters more appealing to girls.

## Work

I have worked as a Junior Incident Responder at [eye Security](https://www.eye.security) in the Hague (2021-2022). I have also been employed as a Teaching Assistant where I assisted in teaching Software Architecture, Software Engineering Methods and Computational Intelligence courses (2019-2022). Before that, I was involved in the Mentorate program for Computer Science where I helped freshmen get acquainted with university life (2018-2019).
